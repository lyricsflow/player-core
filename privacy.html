Privacy Policy

Transparency regarding what information Lyricsflow processes, retains, and displays.

1. Information We Collect

When you sync lyrics or manage your community profile through our Discord integration:

  • Public Profile Information: We store your chosen public handle, display name, avatar, banner, and pronouns to render creator attribution.
  • Uploaded Content: TTML or JSON lyrics files you contribute for songs.
  • Private IDs: Your raw numeric Discord user ID is kept strictly server-side for author verification and is never published or exposed to browser clients.

2. What We Never Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.
  • We do not track cross-site activities or store behavioral cookies.
  • We do not expose internal user IDs or account tokens in network payloads or URLs.

3. Data Removal & Updates

If you update your Discord username, Lyricsflow automatically routes requests to your new username slug. To request complete removal of synced lyrics or profile data, contact the project maintainers via the Discord server.
